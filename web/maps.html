    About the checks

    A map is fair when every colony has the same terrain: point mirror (two colonies) or tiles (n colonies) make that so by construction, and the checks say whether a map has it. Measure how much a difference matters with the fairness check in the Arena (docs/MATCH.md). The world wraps round its edges; a rock border is only for the contest's engine.
